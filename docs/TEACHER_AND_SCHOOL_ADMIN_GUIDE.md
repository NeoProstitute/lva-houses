# Leonardo V Academy Houses — Teacher and School Administrator Guide

This guide is for everyday use after the school system has been set up.

## Roles

| Role | What the person can do |
| --- | --- |
| Student | View their own points, achievements and category progress; request a password reset. |
| Teacher | Award points to active students and correct a point award they created. |
| School administrator | Manage accounts, houses and point categories; award points; correct any point award. |

## For teachers: awarding points

1. Sign in and open the portal.
2. Use **Find a student** to search by a student’s name or house.
3. Choose the student.
4. Choose a category: Learning, Behaviour, Projects, or Participation.
5. Enter the number of points. The category shows its allowed maximum.
6. Write a clear reason that describes the action being recognised.
7. Select **Award points**.

The award is immediately added to the points ledger. The student’s portal, their house total and the public standings update from the same record.

### Writing a useful reason

Good reasons are short, specific and factual:

- “Prepared a clear explanation for the group’s science experiment.”
- “Helped a new classmate join the activity.”
- “Presented a well-researched project with accurate sources.”

Avoid reasons that are vague, private, or identify another student unnecessarily.

### Correcting a mistake

If a teacher entered an award by mistake, select **Undo** next to that award in the ledger. The original award remains auditable and a correcting entry is recorded. Teachers can correct only awards they created; a school administrator can correct any award.

Do not create a second positive award to compensate for an error. Use the correction action so that the student and house totals remain clear.

## For students

Students sign in with their school account to see:

- total points;
- achievement history and the reason for each award;
- strongest categories;
- categories with room for future growth;
- activity filtered by period.

If a student has forgotten a password, they should use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. A password-reset link is sent to their school email address.

## For school administrators

### Create or update accounts

1. Open **School controls** and select **People**.
2. Search for an existing person or select **Add an account**.
3. Enter the person’s school name, username, school email address, role and a temporary password.
4. For every student, select their house.
5. Ask the person to reset the temporary password through the school email workflow at first sign-in.

Deactivate accounts rather than reusing them for another person. Keep at least two active school administrators.

### Manage houses

Under **Houses**, administrators can update a house name, meaning, symbol, description and colour. Keep names and branding consistent across the school before publishing changes.

### Manage categories

Under **Categories**, administrators can create a category, set its maximum number of points per award, edit it, or mark it inactive. Inactive categories stay visible in historic records but can no longer receive new points.

### House imagery

Administrators can upload a house emblem in PNG, JPEG or WebP format. Use a clean, school-approved image and verify it on desktop and phone after uploading.

## Recommended school process

1. Agree the point categories and usual point ranges before the term begins.
2. Publish a short staff guideline explaining what each category recognises.
3. Encourage teachers to award points promptly and write factual reasons.
4. Review the public standings regularly, but avoid using them as the only measure of student contribution.
5. Review inactive accounts and administrator access once each term.
